Integrated AI Pipeline

Replaces static manual route planning with a dynamic, hazard-aware system for research vessel captains. Connects disparate standalone systems into a cohesive engine.

01
ConvLSTM
Sea-Ice Forecasting
Processes spatiotemporal satellite SIC (Sentinel-1, AMSR2) datasets. Outputs a rolling recursive N-day forecast raster grid for ice concentration.
02
XGBoost
Trajectory Drift
Evaluates complex meteorological variables (wind direction, oceanic currents) via physics-prior delta to predict individual iceberg drift pathways.
03
TD-A* Routing
Decision Engine
Discretizes domain into a (x, y, t) matrix. Converts sea-ice resistance and inflated iceberg safety buffers into edge costs to find the fuel-optimal path.

Feasibility &
Dynamic Adaptation

The Antarctic environment is incredibly volatile, with sea-ice concentration shifting drastically over the annual cycle. Our predictive models are designed to adapt to these massive spatial shifts.

  • Seasonal Volatility: The ConvLSTM module is trained on historical data spanning the entire annual freeze-thaw cycle (as seen in the 2017 variance) to maintain accuracy year-round.
  • Incomplete SAR Coverage: Fuses coarser AMSR2 passive-microwave daily data to fill Sentinel-1 gaps, actively flagging low-confidence grid cells.
  • Erratic Iceberg Drift: Blends XGBoost extrapolation with physics-based drift priors to manage looping and stagnation.

Global Impact

🌍 Environmental

Treats fuel efficiency as an explicit cost variable in the TD-A* algorithm. Fuel-optimized routing directly reduces carbon footprints and harmful black-carbon emissions.

🛡️ Social / Safety

Provides early warnings before hazards are visually detectable. Significantly lowers the risk of vessel-ice collisions, protecting crews and scientists.

🇮🇳 Indigenous Capability

Builds native forecasting capacity. Reduces the Ministry of Earth Sciences' (MoES) dependence on international ice services for mission-critical logistics.
